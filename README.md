# RollerCoaster Tycoon: AYN Thor Dual-Screen Edition 🎢

A dual-screen Android port of the classic **RollerCoaster Tycoon**, designed specifically for the **AYN Thor** handheld.

Built around the OpenRCT2 engine, this project reimagines the original RollerCoaster Tycoon experience for a dual-screen device, retaining the charm, visuals and gameplay of the PC classic whilst introducing a dedicated touch-friendly interface and native handheld controls.

### 🎮 Key Features

- **True Dual-Screen Gameplay:** Your theme park occupies the upper display, whilst menus, construction tools and management interfaces live on the lower touchscreen.
- **Redesigned Touch Interface:** Enlarged, interactive menus make building rides, managing staff and running your park easier on a handheld.
- **Full-Screen Construction Tools:** Rollercoaster, path and track-building interfaces expand across the lower screen, with larger, more accessible controls.
- **Native AYN Thor Controls:** Integrated physical button, analogue stick and touchscreen support, designed to work together seamlessly.
- **Intelligent Menu Scaling:** Compact original PC interfaces are reorganised and enlarged to make better use of the Thor's second display.
- **Context-Sensitive Controls:** Clear selection highlights, intuitive touch interactions and improved construction feedback.
- **Integrated Control Tips:** Helpful AYN Thor control hints appear within the game's information ticker.
- **Authentic RollerCoaster Tycoon Experience:** Classic graphics, sound effects, scenarios, rides and the original game's wonderfully chaotic park management remain intact.

### 🛠️ Built for the Thor

Rather than simply stretching a desktop game across two screens, this edition treats the AYN Thor as a genuine dual-screen platform.

The upper screen remains focused on your bustling theme park, whilst the lower display serves as a dedicated management and construction console. Windows and tools can expand to fill the second screen, making complex tasks such as rollercoaster construction considerably more practical.

The aim is simple: **the original RollerCoaster Tycoon, with a modern dual-screen interface that feels purpose-built for the hardware.**
